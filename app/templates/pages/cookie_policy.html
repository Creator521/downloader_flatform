Cookie Policy

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to provide a better user experience, and to supply reporting information to website operators. Cookies can be "first-party" (set by the website you are visiting) or "third-party" (set by a service used by the website, such as an analytics or advertising provider).

2. How We Use Cookies

SnapReelDownload uses cookies for three main purposes: to ensure the website functions correctly, to understand how visitors use our service, and to serve relevant advertisements that fund our free tool. We do not use cookies to identify you personally, track your download history, or store any sensitive information.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

4. How to Manage and Delete Cookies

You have full control over the cookies stored on your device. Here are several ways to manage them:

  • Browser Settings: Most web browsers allow you to view, manage, and delete cookies through the privacy or settings menu. Consult your browser's help documentation for specific instructions:
    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari (iOS)
    • Safari (macOS)
    • Microsoft Edge
  • Google Ad Personalisation: Opt out of personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.
  • Industry Opt-Out Tools:
    • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) Opt-Out
    • Your Online Choices (EU)
    • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out
  • Google Analytics Opt-Out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-On to prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics on any website.

Important: If you disable or delete cookies, some features of SnapReelDownload may not work as intended — for example, the download status indicator relies on session cookies to communicate between the server and your browser.

5. Do Not Track (DNT) Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting that sends a signal to websites requesting that your browsing activity not be tracked. There is currently no universally accepted standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals. At this time, SnapReelDownload does not alter its data collection practices in response to DNT signals, but we respect your right to use browser-level controls and opt-out tools listed above.
