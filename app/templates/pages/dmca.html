DMCA Policy

Respect for Intellectual Property

SnapReelDownload respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects its users to do the same. We comply with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and respond promptly to any legitimate infringement notices. Our service is designed to facilitate personal, offline viewing of publicly available content, not to enable copyright infringement.

Important: We Do Not Host Content

SnapReelDownload does not store, host, or cache any video, audio, or image files on our servers. Our service acts as a technical intermediary that processes publicly available URLs. All media content resides on the original platform's servers (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, etc.) and is downloaded directly from those servers to the user's device. Therefore, DMCA takedown requests regarding the actual content should be directed to the hosting platform where the content is published.

How to File a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner or authorized agent, and you believe that content accessible through our service infringes upon your copyrights, you may submit a DMCA takedown notice. While we do not host the content itself, we can block specific URLs from being processed by our tool. Your notice must include:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed
  • The specific URL(s) that you wish to have blocked from our processing service
  • Your contact information, including name, address, telephone number, and email address
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf

Response Timeline

Upon receiving a valid DMCA takedown notice, we will review and respond within 48-72 business hours. If the notice is complete and valid, we will take appropriate action, which may include blocking the reported URL(s) from being processed by our service. We take all legitimate takedown requests seriously and strive to resolve them as quickly as possible.

Counter-Notification

If you believe that your content was removed or blocked in error, you may file a counter-notification with the required information under the DMCA. Counter-notifications must be submitted in writing and include your contact details, the specific URL(s) that were blocked, and a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed by mistake.

Repeat Infringers

We reserve the right to terminate access for users who are found to be repeat copyright infringers. We maintain records of valid DMCA notices and may take additional measures against users who repeatedly violate copyright laws through our service.
