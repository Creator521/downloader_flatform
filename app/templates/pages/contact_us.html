Quick Answers Before You Write

These are the most common questions we get — your answer might already be here.

Why is my download not working?
The most common reasons: the account is private, the video was deleted, or you copied an incomplete URL. Try opening the video in a browser and copying the full URL from the address bar.
Can I download videos from private accounts?
No. We can only download publicly available content. Private accounts, age-restricted videos, and DRM-protected content cannot be accessed by any web-based downloader.
Is SnapReelDownload really free?
Yes, completely free. No hidden charges, no premium plans, no daily limits. We're funded by non-intrusive ads, not by charging our users.
I found a DMCA violation — what do I do?
We take copyright seriously. Email us at support@snapreeldownload.com with details and we'll respond within 48 hours.
