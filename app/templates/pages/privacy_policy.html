Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

SnapReelDownload is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not require you to create an account, provide your email address, or share any personal information to use our video downloading service. We do not collect usernames, passwords, or social media credentials at any point during the download process.

We may automatically collect limited, non-personal technical information such as your browser type, operating system, language preference, referring website URL, and general geographic region. This data is collected through standard web server logs and analytics tools solely for the purpose of improving our service and understanding usage patterns.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and serve relevant advertisements through our advertising partners. The types of cookies we use include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the basic operation of the website, such as session management and security features
  • Analytics cookies: Used by Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, and navigation patterns
  • Advertising cookies: Used by Google AdSense and other advertising partners to serve personalized ads based on your browsing interests and behavior across the web

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate and improve our website:

  • Google Analytics: For website traffic analysis and user behavior insights. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymized data about your interactions with our site
  • Google AdSense: For serving relevant advertisements. AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to personalize the ads shown to you based on your browsing history

These third-party services have their own privacy policies governing the collection and use of your data. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of Google and any other services you interact with through our website.

4. Data Storage and Security

We do not store any downloaded video or audio files on our servers. All downloads are processed in real-time and delivered directly to your device. We do not maintain logs of the specific URLs you download, the content of the videos you save, or any personal download history. Our website uses SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS) to ensure that all communications between your browser and our servers are secure and encrypted.

5. Children's Privacy

SnapReelDownload is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate action.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at: contact@snapreeldownload.com
