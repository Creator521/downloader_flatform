Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

SnapReelDownload is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not require you to create an account, provide your email address, or share any personal information to use our video downloading service. We do not collect usernames, passwords, or social media credentials at any point during the download process.

We may automatically collect limited, non-personal technical information such as your browser type, operating system, language preference, referring website URL, and general geographic region. This data is collected through standard web server logs and analytics tools solely for the purpose of improving our service and understanding usage patterns.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and serve relevant advertisements through our advertising partners. For a comprehensive explanation of every cookie we set, its purpose, and its retention period, please see our dedicated Cookie Policy.

The types of cookies we use include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the basic operation of the website, such as session management and security features. These cookies are strictly necessary and cannot be disabled without affecting site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4): Used to understand how visitors interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, and navigation patterns. GA4 cookies are retained for up to 14 months.
  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense): Used by Google AdSense to serve personalised ads based on your browsing interests and behaviour across the web. These cookies may be retained for up to 13 months.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. You may also opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings or the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We use the following third-party services to operate and improve our website:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): For website traffic analysis and user behaviour insights. GA4 uses first-party cookies to collect anonymised, aggregated data about your interactions with our site. Data is processed by Google LLC in the United States. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google AdSense: For serving relevant advertisements. AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to personalise the ads shown to you based on your browsing history. Google acts as a data processor in this context. More information: Google advertising policies.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not share any user data with external parties except as required by the third-party services listed above or as required by law.

4. Data Storage, Retention and Security

We do not store any downloaded video or audio files on our servers. All downloads are processed in real-time and delivered directly to your device. We do not maintain logs of the specific URLs you download, the content of the videos you save, or any personal download history.

Retention periods:

  • Server access logs (IP addresses, request timestamps): retained for 7 days, then automatically purged.
  • Analytics data (aggregated, anonymised): retained for 14 months within Google Analytics, then automatically deleted.
  • Advertising cookies: retained for up to 13 months by Google AdSense.
  • We do not store any user-generated content, download history, or personally identifiable information beyond the periods stated above.

Our website uses SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS) to ensure that all communications between your browser and our servers are secure and encrypted. We implement Content Security Policy (CSP) headers, HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), and other industry-standard security measures to protect against common web vulnerabilities.

5. International Data Transfers

Our servers are hosted in the United States. If you access SnapReelDownload from outside the United States — including from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or any other jurisdiction — your technical data (browser type, IP address in server logs) may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States.

Google LLC, our analytics and advertising partner, participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and has committed to adhering to appropriate safeguards for the processing of personal data transferred from the EEA and UK. By using our website, you acknowledge and consent to the transfer of your data as described in this section.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request that we delete any personal data we hold about you, subject to legal obligations.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Right to Object: You may object to the processing of your data for direct marketing or profiling purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@snapreeldownload.com. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

7. Your Rights Under CCPA (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you the following rights:

  • Right to Know: You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: You may request that we delete any personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information. No opt-out is necessary.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

To submit a request, please email us at contact@snapreeldownload.com. We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days.

8. Children's Privacy

SnapReelDownload is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate action to delete that information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page.
